# Custom signs on the booking page

Status: **IDEA (recorded 2026-10-22)**. Not at launch.

Agencies buy signs outside the platform library and upload them
(Signs > Custom). They can be stocked today, but booking can't place them:
the layout uses the platform sign art.

- Needs a format check on upload (cut-out PNG, size, maybe an editor or AI review).
- Needs a way to put a custom sign into a display, e.g. as a decoration.
- Since 2026-10-25 platform decorations are full-color art in the manifest
  (style "art", ARCHITECTURE.md "Zone 3/4/5 extras"); a custom sign could
  follow the same shape: one image, a size in inches, a baseline.
